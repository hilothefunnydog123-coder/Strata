Comprehensive Genomic Profiling in Oncology (0123)

Coverage Policy

Cigna covers tumor comprehensive genomic profiling using next-generation sequencing as medically necessary when the individual has advanced or metastatic solid tumor cancer and the ordering oncologist attests that results will directly inform selection of therapy.

Coverage is limited to one comprehensive genomic profiling assay per primary tumor diagnosis, unless there is documented disease progression.

Cigna covers testing only when performed by a laboratory accredited under CLIA and, where applicable, by the College of American Pathologists.

Not Covered

Cigna does not cover comprehensive genomic profiling for population screening in asymptomatic individuals, as clinical utility for this indication is not established.
