Comprehensive Genomic Profiling for Advanced Solid Tumors (ONC-22)

Coverage

Humana covers comprehensive genomic profiling as medically necessary for members with advanced or metastatic solid tumor cancer when the treating physician will use the results to guide selection of anti-cancer therapy.

Coverage requires that the test be performed in a CLIA-certified laboratory and that documentation of the intended use of the results accompany the order.

Limitations

Humana considers comprehensive genomic profiling investigational for use in monitoring minimal residual disease, as the clinical utility for this use has not been demonstrated in prospective outcome studies.
