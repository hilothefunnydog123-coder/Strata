Molecular Profiling to Guide Therapy in Solid Tumors (ONC.045)

Coverage Rationale

Comprehensive molecular profiling is proven and medically necessary for members with advanced or metastatic solid tumors when the results will be used to guide systemic anti-cancer therapy.

The test must be performed in a CLIA-certified laboratory and ordered by the physician managing the member's oncologic care.

Comprehensive molecular profiling is unproven and not medically necessary for monitoring of treatment response using circulating tumor DNA, because the clinical utility has not been established in prospective controlled studies.

Definitions

Advanced disease refers to Stage III or Stage IV malignancy per AJCC staging. This definition is provided for clarity and does not itself impose a coverage requirement.
