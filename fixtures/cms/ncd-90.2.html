National Coverage Determination (NCD 90.2): Next Generation Sequencing for Patients with Advanced Cancer

Indications and Limitations of Coverage

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services covers diagnostic laboratory tests using next generation sequencing when performed in a CLIA-certified laboratory, when ordered by a treating physician, and when the patient has the following:

  • either recurrent, relapsed, refractory, metastatic, or advanced Stage III or IV cancer; and
  • has not been previously tested using the same NGS test for the same primary diagnosis of cancer unless a new primary cancer diagnosis is made by the treating physician; and
  • has decided to seek further cancer treatment.

The diagnostic laboratory test using next generation sequencing must have an FDA approval or clearance as a companion in vitro diagnostic, and its use must be consistent with the FDA-approved or FDA-cleared indications.

Other Applications

For patients with advanced cancer whose test does not meet the companion diagnostic criteria above, coverage of next generation sequencing may be determined by the local Medicare Administrative Contractor.
