Comprehensive Genomic Profiling in Advanced Cancer (CG-GENE-14)

Clinical Indications

Comprehensive genomic profiling is considered medically necessary when the individual has advanced-stage or metastatic solid tumor malignancy and the result is expected to change oncologic management.

Testing must be limited to one comprehensive panel per primary cancer, and must be ordered by or in consultation with a board-certified oncologist.

Not Medically Necessary

Comprehensive genomic profiling is considered not medically necessary when performed for screening of asymptomatic individuals or when the result will not affect treatment selection.
