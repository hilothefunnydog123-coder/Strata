Genomic Sequence Analysis Panels for Solid Tumors (506.081)

Medical Policy Statement

Blue Cross Blue Shield of Michigan considers genomic sequence analysis panels established for members with advanced or metastatic solid tumors when used to select targeted systemic therapy.

The panel must be performed by a laboratory holding CLIA certification, and testing is covered once per primary tumor diagnosis absent documented progression.

Exclusions

Repeat panel testing performed within the same line of therapy without evidence of progression is considered not established and is excluded from coverage.
